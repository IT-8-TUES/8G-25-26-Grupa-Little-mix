The Sound of Bulgarian Tradition

Explore the rich world of Bulgarian traditional instruments. From the magical sound of the gaida to the deep rhythm of the tupan, Bulgarian folklore music carries centuries of history.

Popular Bulgarian Instruments

Gaida

A traditional Bulgarian bagpipe known for its powerful and emotional sound.

Kaval

A wooden flute with a soft and warm tone, associated with shepherd traditions.

Gadulka

A bowed string instrument that creates distinctive folk melodies.

Tupan

A large traditional drum used during festivals, dances, and celebrations.
