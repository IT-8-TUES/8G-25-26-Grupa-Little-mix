The Sound of Bulgarian Tradition

Explore the rich world of Bulgarian traditional instruments. From the magical sound of the gaida to the deep rhythm of the tupan, Bulgarian folklore music carries centuries of history, culture, and national spirit.

Popular Bulgarian Instruments

Gaida

The gaida is a traditional Bulgarian bagpipe known for its powerful and emotional sound, commonly used in folk music.

Kaval

The kaval is a wooden flute with a soft and warm tone, often associated with shepherd traditions and Bulgarian folklore.

Gadulka

The gadulka is a bowed string instrument that creates distinctive folk melodies and traditional harmonies.

Tupan

The tupan is a large traditional drum used to create energetic rhythms during festivals, dances, and celebrations.
