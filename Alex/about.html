The Sound of Bulgaria

Bulgarian music is full of emotion, tradition, powerful vocals and unforgettable rhythms.

Traditional Bulgarian Folklore

Traditional Bulgarian folklore is one of the oldest and most unique musical traditions in Europe. It is known for its powerful vocals, unusual rhythms such as 7/8 and 9/8, and traditional instruments like the gaida, kaval, and tambura. Different regions of Bulgaria have their own folk dances, costumes, and music styles that reflect local culture and history.

Bulgarian Folk Dances by Regions

Ravnodelni Horo are dances with even rhythms like 2/4 and 4/4.

• Pravo Horo – all regions of Bulgaria

• Dunavsko Horo – Northern Bulgaria

• Selsko Horo – Shopska Region
